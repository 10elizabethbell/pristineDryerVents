# Product: Pristine Dryer Vents LLC website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page local-service site)

## Users
- Homeowners in Ocean County, NJ (Manchester, Whiting, Lakehurst first, per the owners' caption) and the wider service area the flyer prints (Bayville, Beachwood, Toms River, Brick, Point Pleasant area, Freehold, Ramtown; "everywhere except North Jersey").
- They arrive on a phone, mostly from Facebook (group posts, shared links), often inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- *Inferred:* Manchester and Whiting are home to large 55+ communities, so a good share of customers are likely older homeowners.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook visitor into a booked dryer vent cleaning by text or call, and give the owners a real web presence they don't have today.

## Positioning
A local, owner-run dryer vent cleaning business (Daniel & Brandi). The hook is fire safety, in their own words: "Clean dryer, no fire."

## Capabilities and constraints
- One service: dryer vent cleaning. Published price $139 per dryer; first-time customers save $10 ($129) (flyer and caption, 2026-10-20).
- Contact: call or text 732-799-1379. No email, no booking system, no business Facebook page, no Google listing.
- Hours, years in business, insurance details: unknown.

## Brand commitments
- Their words: "Clean dryer, no fire."; "Safer homes. Lower drying times. Peace of mind."; "Locally owned & insured"; "Friendly, reliable service".
- Colours from their flyer logo: bright vent blue (#15a0f5), deep navy, white and silver.
- Tone: casual and neighborly ("Hey neighbors! We're Daniel & Brandi…").

## Evidence on hand
- One promo flyer (AI-style illustration, not job photos). Its before/after is an illustration, not a photographed job.
- No reviews. The flyer's "5 star reviews on Google" claim is unverified and is not used.

## Principles
- Facts only; owner's voice ("we").
- Phone first; the first screen shows the offer and the text button.
- Never merge with the unrelated "Pristine Dryer Vent…" companies or Dryer Vent Wizard.
